# Threshold Deep — Session Log, 2026-08-02 → 08-03

*One long day/night. The necromancer roster went from an idea in acts.md
to three playable variants, the dungeon learned to fight itself much
harder, and kills stopped being the headline number. Written down because
most of what's below is **decisions with reasons** — the code is
self-evident, the reasoning isn't.*

Companion docs written the same session: **docs/stations.md** (walk-around
props from 2D faces) and **docs/necromancers.md** (the roster's asset +

## What shipped

### Audio & music

- **Per-world music sets.** `assets/audio/music/dry|damp|deep/` mirroring
  the tile appearances, plus `boss/`. `WORLD_SET` in `music.gd` is indexed
  by world exactly like `dungeon.gd`'s `WORLD_APPEARANCE`; `boss/` is
  keyed to floor **kind** instead and overrides the world on every boss
  floor. Preload arrays are the contract, not the folders — runtime
  `res://` scanning doesn't survive an export.
- **A boss fight owns its music.** `MusicDrift.take_over()` on the consent
  plate (drift loop bails, one looping track from the top, no random
  silences), `duck()` on the 3-3 floor drop, `release()` on clear.
- **The launch cue.** `cast_sound` fires from `_fire_orb` for every
  element — closes creature-polish.md's outstanding wizard item.
- **Dash impact sounds.** `dash_bump1.ogg` / `barrel_strike1.ogg`.
- **Plank creak.** `wood_floor_creek.ogg` under the player *and* under any
  creature crossing boards.

### Combat feel

- **Skeleton rise screenshake** — `_rise_shake()`, distance-attenuated,
  landing on the rattle rather than the stand, with the revenant pack
  hitting harder than a single restless pile.
- **Melee recoil** — a landed hit shoves you back by weapon heft
  (`MELEE_RECOIL`: torch 1.2 / sword 2.0 / halberd 3.2 m/s).
- **Dash contact** — camera shake on a dash into a body, bigger with
  Barrelstone.

### The necromancers

- **`wizard.gd` is element-driven**: `enum Element { BLUE, RED, BROWN }`,
  per-element const blocks, `_apply_element()` in `_ready()`, rolled in
  `setup()`. One script for the whole roster (the mush/slime pattern).
- **`orb.gd` is fully parameterized**: `glow_color`, `glow_energy`,
  `flight_sound`, `dot_kind` join the existing frames/impacts/damage.
- **Red** — fireball, Ember, orange glow, 19 sprites + 8 sounds.
- **Brown** — conjured debris orb, no DoT, dim amber glow, 19 + 8.
- **`kill_label()` → "Necromancer"** while the code family stays `wizard`.

### The dungeon fights itself

- **Slime creep is an active hazard** for creatures, not just the player —
  it was literally a Sprite3D with no logic ("Cosmetic for now").
- **Infight rallies** — a creature struck by another creature now shouts,
  and idle neighbours pile onto the aggressor.
- **Ember on the player** — `take_burn()` + `burned` signal + orange HUD
  pulse, the twin of `take_poison`.

### Systems & meta

- **The early boss-floor drop** — fall through an arena plank mid-wave and
  the floor caves *then*, with the living wave riding it down to finish the
  fight in the chamber.
- **Spoils fall with the floor** — combat drops joined a `"drops"` group.
- **Upper wall band is a tell** — `wall_upper2` marks boss arenas and item
  rooms; everything else wears `wall_upper1`.
- **Score replaces kills as the headline**, plus a run clock. HUD reads
  `floor · time · score`; reports lead with SCORE.

## Decisions worth remembering

**Boss music starts at the consent plate, not the floor drop.** The plate
is the single entry point for all three boss floors; the drop only exists
on 3-3. Starting at the plate also means the player's *choice* is what the
music answers, and it leaves the drop's boom uncontested (the drop gets a
duck instead of an onset).

**Time is shown but never scored.** These levels reward slowing down —
bone piles that stir when you linger, a secret whose only tell is a pale
plank, item rooms that respect leaving empty-handed. A speed bonus would
score against the level design.

**Kills are weighted low in the score.** Only player kills count, and the
dungeon now fights itself constantly. A kill-heavy score would punish
letting the deep do the work, which is the best thing it does.

**Melee recoil fires on connect only, and adds to input velocity rather
than replacing it.** Recoil on a whiff reads as a bug; a stagger on the
player reads as losing control. It also finally gives the halberd a real
cost — CLAUDE.md admitted it "trades nothing for reach."

**Shake the dash, never the passive bump.** A bump is *continuous*
contact; it would rattle the screen the whole time an enemy crowds you and
stop meaning "impact" within seconds. Screenshake needs a discrete cause.

**A projectile's glow is dimmed, never killed.** The dungeon is lit by
your torch alone, so a projectile throwing no light can't be seen coming.
Pillar 3 beats realism — that's why brown's "rock" still glows.

**The debris orb beat the thrown rock.** It deletes the ballistic-arc
requirement entirely, fits necromancers who transmute matter far better
than a bandit throwing a stone, keeps all three sharing one telegraph
grammar (charge glow → orb, read the colour), and turns the glow from a
compromise into the obvious thing conjured matter would do.

**Ember takes two routes.** Creatures get a `Dot` node; the player gets
`take_burn`. `Dot` ticks through `host.take_damage`, which on the player
runs i-frames — burns would have been swallowed at random and each
survivor would read as a fresh blow.

**Kin never rally against their own script.** Without the guard, a slime
rotting a skeleton would also wake *other slimes* and set them on their own
kind. `e.get_script() == against.get_script()` covers every family in one
line.

**Creak punch came from contrast, not gain.** Two loops in one frequency
band mask each other; ducking the footsteps on planks opened ~8 dB of
space where pushing the creak alone just made the mix louder.

**Rename a file only when the bad name will sit next to correct ones and
get copied.** Fixed `projectile-wizard.ogg` and `frogmen_aggro.ogg`;
deferred `sprites/skeletal-wizard/` (32 refs, can't propagate, and
verifying it means playing to 3-3); declined the eight unnumbered death
sounds outright. The convention binds new files, not old ones.

## Gotchas found the hard way

These cost real debugging time and will again if forgotten:

- **`setup()` runs BEFORE `add_child()`** — `@onready` nodes are null
  there. The element is *rolled* in `setup()` and *applied* in `_ready()`.
- **`_dress_upper_walls()` ran before `arena_room_idx` was assigned** — it
  would have silently painted the whole floor plain. Moved beside
  `_vary_ceilings()`, which depends on the same indices.
- **Children `_ready` before their parent** — `FlightSound`'s `autoplay`
  started the *default* stream a beat before `orb.gd` could swap it, and
  assigning `stream` to a playing player stops it dead. Autoplay came off
  the scene; the script starts it explicitly.
- **Creature `FALL_Y` was a `const`** — living bodies couldn't survive the
  boss drop. `skeleton.gd`/`wizard.gd` now carry `var fall_y`, matching
  `skeletal_wizard.gd`. Slime/mush/frogman still don't.
- **Corpses have no physics** (`_physics_process` returns on `dead`), so
  they hang in the air over a caved floor unless tweened down.
- **`_arena_has_living_enemies()` tests XZ only** — which is exactly why
  the early drop works with no re-sequencing: the chamber is directly
  below, so "in the arena" stays true 12 m down.
- **`STEP_DB_*` overwrite `$StepSound`'s scene volume every frame** —
  tuning it in `player.tscn` does nothing.
- **`is` on a freed instance is an ERROR, not `false`.** Caught the infight
  rally in playtest (`dungeon.gd:364`): a creature holds its grudge target
  until that target *dies*, so `e.get("target")` is routinely a freed
  reference. `if t is PhysicsBody3D and is_instance_valid(t)` crashes —
  **`is_instance_valid()` must come first.** Any check on a reference held
  across frames needs validity before type; a check on a freshly-passed
  argument doesn't.
